### Iran Introduces Bitcoin-Based Shipping Insurance
**Source:** [https://coinpedia.org/news/iran-launches-bitcoin-shipping-insurance-platform-hormuz-safe/](https://coinpedia.org/news/iran-launches-bitcoin-shipping-insurance-platform-hormuz-safe/)

**Summary:** 
Iran has launched 'Hormuz Safe,' a Bitcoin-powered maritime insurance platform, aimed at facilitating the insurance process for cargo operators navigating through the Persian Gulf and the Strait of Hormuz. The platform allows operators to purchase maritime insurance using Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies, bypassing traditional financial systems. There are concerns regarding the legality and acceptance of the insurance certificates issued, as well as the risk of facing secondary sanctions from U.S. regulators. This initiative reflects a broader trend towards using digital currencies in international trade and geopolitics, highlighting a shift towards de-dollarization and the integration of Bitcoin into real-world applications.
