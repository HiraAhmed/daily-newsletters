### Iran Introduces Bitcoin-Based Shipping Insurance
**Source:** [https://coinpedia.org/news/iran-launches-bitcoin-shipping-insurance-platform-hormuz-safe/](https://coinpedia.org/news/iran-launches-bitcoin-shipping-insurance-platform-hormuz-safe/)

**Summary:** 
Iran has initiated a Bitcoin-powered maritime insurance platform, 'Hormuz Safe', to facilitate insurance processes for cargo operators in the Persian Gulf and the Strait of Hormuz. The platform allows operators to purchase insurance using Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies, bypassing traditional financial systems and potentially circumventing Western sanctions and the dollar-based SWIFT system. Despite potential revenue generation, there are concerns about the legality and acceptance of the issued insurance certificates and the risk of secondary sanctions from U.S. regulators. This move reflects a broader trend towards the use of digital currencies in international trade and geopolitics, indicating a shift towards de-dollarization and the integration of Bitcoin into real-world applications.
